Why book Critical Wits!?

  • Proven on the festival circuit

    Our show and our cast come from the gritty streets and stages of the Renaissance festival circuit: the Colorado Renaissance Festival and the Santa Fe Renaissance Festival.

  • Narrative comedy, RPG-style

    Critical Wits! is narrative comedy improv that draws heavily on classic role-playing games - tabletop and video game alike: Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, Baldur's Gate, Call of Cthulhu, and more. Audience prompts launch the story, and audience interaction drives it all the way through.

  • Tailored to your event

    Critical Wits! is available for festivals, conventions, clubs and private events throughout the region. Every performance is tailored to its audience and venue.
