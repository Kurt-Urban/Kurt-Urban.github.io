Our main show

A live, improvised D&D-style adventure where the audience builds the heroes and the villain, then rolls the dice.

  1. 1

    Draft the party

    The audience decides who our two heroes and our villain are. Names, backgrounds, fatal flaws: you call it.

  2. 2

    Raid the costume chest

    Each character gets dressed from our chest of costumes and props. Fashion choices are final.

  3. 3

    Arm the heroes

    One hero gets a weapon, the other gets magic, and you pick both. A pool noodle? Necromancy for houseplants? Absolutely.

  4. 4

    Give the villain a past

    The audience writes the villain's backstory. The more tragic, the better.

  5. 5

    Roll for it

    Once the quest begins, you're still in it. Audience members roll for ability checks and cast spells, and the dice decide what happens next.

Who we areWho we are

Wit's End is an improv comedy troupe that builds every show from scratch with the audience. Our main show, Critical Wits!, turns your suggestions into a full fantasy adventure that happens exactly once.

No D&D experience required. If you've never rolled a die in your life, you'll still know exactly who to root for (and who to boo).
