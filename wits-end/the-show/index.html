Critical Wits!

The Show

The Critical Wits! Show is rooted in audience interaction - that's our renaissance festival foundation showing.

While entirely customizable, it usually goes like this...

- after introducing the show and players, we hand out Spell Cards to willing audience members. They're told that, at any time, they may raise their hand, read the spell, and cast it. You might see a major character suddenly polymorphed into a tiny goblin or a member of Nsync summoned to fight for the Villain... you never know.

- each show has a Game Master/ Dungeon Master-narrator who takes audience prompts, finds opportunities for the audience to make decisions (usually using a D20), and adjudicates spells.

- there is usually a Villain, and the audience decides their evil motivations and powers. The Villain may have henchmen, who carry out the Villain's evil plans.

- there are usually a couple of Heroes and the audience decides their capabilities and reasons for adventuring.

- NPCs fill in the rest - from random encounters, to furniture, to ye olde bartenders giving out story exposition.

- from these these components, our performers craft a story of adventure, combat, betrayal, love, absurdity, heroism, and whatever else pops into their minds.

- no two shows are even close to the same and they can run from 30-45 minutes to much-much longer.

- most of our shows are base-line family-friendly (PG), but we can both increase the "adult-ness" to R-rated and decrease it for younger audiences.

Show descriptions

Each description may be used as written or shortened as needed.

  • One line

  • Short (about 50 words)

    Critical Wits! is the RPG improv show where the audience rolls dice, casts spells, and decides the story.   • Full (about 140 words)

    Critical Wits! is the RPG improv show where the audience rolls dice, casts spells, and decides the story.

    Developed on the streets and stages of the Renaissance festival circuit, including the Colorado Renaissance Festival and the Santa Fe Renaissance Festival, Critical Wits! is narrative comedy improv that draws heavily on classic role-playing games, from tabletop favorites like Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder and Call of Cthulhu to video games like Baldur's Gate.

    Nothing is scripted and nothing is rehearsed. The audience's prompts launch the adventure, and audience members roll the dice and cast the spells that steer it all the way to the finale. No gaming experience is required: longtime players will recognize every reference, and every audience member takes part in a heroic, comedic story of their own making.

    Critical Wits! is a production of Wit's End Improv and is available for festivals, conventions, clubs and private events.
