Questions

Does anyone need to know D&D?

Not at all. Fans will catch the references, and when it's your turn to roll for an ability check or cast a spell, we'll walk you through it.

Can the show be clean?

Yes. Tell us about your audience and we'll keep it family-friendly, office-friendly, or anywhere in between.

How much space do you need?

A clear area about the size of a living room works. A stage, microphones and lights are a bonus, not a requirement.

Can you tailor the show to our event?

Absolutely. We can theme the quest around your event, cast your guest of honor as the villain, and keep the spotlight off anyone who'd rather just watch.

How far ahead should we book?

A month or more is ideal, but reach out even if your date is sooner.
