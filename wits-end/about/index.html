History

The story of Critical Wits! and the troupe behind it.

Who we are

We grew up on the gritty streets of the Colorado Renaissance Festival.

Our stage was a yellow rope in a circle on the gravel, with a red cart and a banner as a backdrop. We played 360 degrees, no microphones, with our audience standing in the sun. Just members of the general cast of the Festival.

At the Festival, we’re known as the Crossroads Theatre Players, because we began by performing on the dirt at the main crossroads near the front gate.

You learn a ton performing like that. If you aren’t entertaining, if your stories aren’t captivating, if your audience doesn’t engage… they just wander off to find a turkey leg, see a joust, or go shopping.

Occasionally, we’d be invited to perform on a primary stage. And when well-known national acts would come to us saying, “Boy, you guys are really funny,” we knew we might have something.

Then we were invited to have our own stage… so long as we built it. So we did.

Now you can see us in June, July, and August on the freshly built, Crossroads Theatre Stage! And, you can book us during the rest of the year for your private event, Con, or venue!

AUDIENCE: Um... ok... then what is Crossroads Theatre Players? NARRATOR: Even better question, good for you.
