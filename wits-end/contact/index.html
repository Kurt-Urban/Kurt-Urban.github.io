Questions

Does the audience need to know RPGs?

Not at all. Gamers will catch every reference, and when it's someone's turn to roll the dice or cast a spell, we walk them through it.

Can you tailor the show to our audience?

Yes. Tell us about your crowd and your venue, and we'll shape the show to fit, from family-friendly to grown-ups only.

What do you need from the venue?

A clear performance area. A stage, microphones and lights are a bonus, not a requirement. We'll go over the details when we plan your show.

How far ahead should we book?

A month or more is ideal, but reach out even if your date is sooner.
