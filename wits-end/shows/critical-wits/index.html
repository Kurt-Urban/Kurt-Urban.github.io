How a quest worksHow a quest works

  1. 1

    Draft the party

    The audience decides who our two heroes and our villain are. Names, backgrounds, fatal flaws: you call it.

  2. 2

    Raid the costume chest

    Each character gets dressed from our chest of costumes and props. Fashion choices are final.

  3. 3

    Arm the heroes

    One hero gets a weapon, the other gets magic, and you pick both. A pool noodle? Necromancy for houseplants? Absolutely.

  4. 4

    Give the villain a past

    The audience writes the villain's backstory. The more tragic, the better.

  5. 5

    Roll for it

    Once the quest begins, you're still in it. Audience members roll for ability checks and cast spells, and the dice decide what happens next.

Now see it with a room full of people yelling

Critical Wits! is a fantasy adventure that doesn’t exist until you show up. Inspired by Dungeons & Dragons, every show starts with an empty party and a room full of suggestions.

You draft our two heroes and our villain. You dress them from our costume chest, arm one hero with a weapon and the other with magic, and give the villain a backstory worth getting revenge over. Then the quest begins, and the cast improvises the whole adventure live, all the way to the final showdown.

And you don’t just watch. Throughout the show, audience members roll for ability checks and cast spells, so the dice (and you) decide what happens as much as we do. Nat 20s are celebrated. Nat 1s are celebrated louder.

No D&D experience required. Longtime players will catch every reference, and everyone else gets a ridiculous, heroic story with characters they built themselves.
